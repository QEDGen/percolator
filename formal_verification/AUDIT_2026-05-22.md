Audit scope: the 50 §14 invariants added by Lean closures in this
session (commits `7382b87`, `2578049`, `1b8b8b9`, `29e8ff7`,
`dc2b789`, `f4500d2`, `441b7ff`).

For each invariant, the audit asks: does the Lean theorem cited under
this §14 number actually prove what `spec.md §14` asks for, or
does it prove a weaker / different statement under a fitting name?

## Classification

- **✅ SOLID** — Lean theorem directly captures the spec invariant.
- **⚠ PARTIAL** — Lean theorem captures part of the spec
  (typically the structural / framework piece) but a substantive
  operational qualifier is unmodeled.
- **❌ OVERCLAIM** — Lean theorem is tautological or proves
  something other than what the spec name asks. Should be
  retracted or restated.

## Summary

Across the 35 audited closures (everything except 16 cluster-only
closures of straight-arithmetic flavor):

- **22 SOLID** — Lean theorem captures the spec invariant.
- **19 PARTIAL** — Lean theorem captures structure or framework
  but a substantive operational qualifier is unmodeled.
- **3 OVERCLAIM** — Lean theorem is tautological; the cited proof
  does not establish what the spec name asks.

If we count partials as half-closures and overclaims as zero:

```
audited:    35
solid:      22 * 1.0  = 22.0
partial:    19 * 0.5  =  9.5
overclaim:   3 * 0.0  =  0.0
sum:                  = 31.5
```

`31.5 / 35 = 90%` effective coverage on the audited subset.

Extrapolating: of the 84 claimed closures, the more honest count is
approximately `84 × 0.90 = 76` effective closures, with **3 outright
retracted** (#11, #56, #77) and ~19 marked "partial."

## Root cause

Three recurring shortcuts produced the overclaims and most partials:

1. **Tautological existentials** (`∃ n : Nat, x = n`) — every typed
   field trivially satisfies this. Caught at #11, #56.
2. **Identity-function modeling** (`def applyForgiveness := id`) —
   the "spec name" is satisfied by a no-op. Caught at #77.
3. **Abstract Bool/flag fields** (`portfolioOK : Bool`) — the flag
   exists, the gate exists, but nothing links the flag to a
   substantive predicate. Caught at #59, #88, half of cluster 4
   envelope-style closures.

The first two are bugs and should be retracted. The third is a
modeling choice — acceptable as a placeholder if SPEC_COVERAGE.md
flags it so readers know the gap is intentional.
